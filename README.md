# ✦ Slotly

**Booking and business management platform for service-based businesses.**

Slotly is a full-stack web application for managing appointments, staff, services, schedules, clients, and daily business operations.

It is designed for:
- solo professionals
- independent specialists
- studios and salons
- multi-location businesses

---

## Overview

Slotly helps service businesses manage bookings in one place, while allowing customers to book available time slots online in a simple way.

The product is inspired by real booking workflows used in modern service businesses, but it is being designed and built as an independent project.

---

## Core idea

Slotly is built around the way booking actually works:

`Service → Location → Staff → Date → Available time → Client details → Booking`

Available time slots are calculated based on:
- staff availability
- time off
- existing bookings
- service duration

---

## Main goals

- make booking simple for clients
- give businesses a clear workspace for daily operations
- support different business models in one system
- prevent scheduling conflicts
- build a product that can scale with business growth

---

## Business models supported

- solo professional working independently
- specialist renting a chair or room
- small salon or studio
- salon with receptionist/admin
- multi-location service business

---

## Planned features

- staff and roles
- services and pricing
- working schedules and time off
- online booking
- manual booking
- rescheduling and cancellation
- client profiles and booking history
